# Cadence

Synced lyrics for your Mac's lock screen, styled like a lyric edit. While music plays in Spotify, Apple Music or
YouTube (in Chrome, Safari, Arc, Brave, Edge or Vivaldi) and the Mac is locked, Cadence fills the screen with the song's lyrics, animated word by word as they're sung,
over a moving wallpaper made from the album art that hits on the beat.

## Features

**Lyrics**
- **17 word animations** — comet, neon, glitch, slam, scramble, cascade, flip, highlighter, shine, reveal,
  bounce, bloom, typewriter, karaoke fill, wave, pop, fade — or **Mix**, a different one every line with the
  punchy ones saved for the chorus.
- **Layouts** — a word stack (one word at a time, drifting in 3D), classic lines with 8 line transitions, or a
  **constellation**, where sung words become stars joined up across the screen, or **fisheye**, a column of words
  rolling under a glass lens, with or without icons, or the looks from live-wallpaper edits: a **column** of the
  line with the sung word boxed, one **big word** that streaks in and breaks into pixels on the way out,
  **extruded** 3D letters, or the sung word on a tilted strip of **tape**. **Matched** gives each visual below the lyric look it comes with.
- **Keyword bursts** — sung words set off effects: "fire" throws up embers, "rain" brings a storm, "love"
  floats hearts, plus stars, lightning, money and snow. Works for Hindi and Telugu words too.
- **Title card** — a new song's title slams in like a music video's opening.
- **Kinetic styles** — seventeen more ways for the line to move: the line orbiting the sung word on a
  ring, stairs, a colour split, scattered words with a framed one, a wavy underline, echo trails, a drawn circle,
  hinged caps, a sparkle trail, confetti, a giant marquee, pills, a word too big for the screen, a 3D twist,
  stamps, thin & bold, and a card that bends to the beat.
- **Colours** — keep each style's own, or take them from the album art or your own colour in one of six
  harmonies (source, tonal, analogous, complementary, split complementary, triadic).
- **A different style on other displays**, and any style for the floating desktop lyrics too.
- **Edit Lyrics…** — fix a line or a timestamp, paste in your own, or import a .lrc or .txt file. Your version
  is used for that song from then on; Use Downloaded Lyrics goes back.
- **Hindi/Telugu in English letters**, spelled the way people type them ("premapichchi", not "premapicci").

**Backgrounds**
- **Album art, moving** — motion wallpaper, high-contrast black & white, floating cover, duotone, spinning vinyl, kaleidoscope, polaroid, cassette, moonlit lake, cinema, retro grid, gallery, city billboard, old TV, deep space, ocean, mountains,
  highway, neon wall, aquarium, forest, code rain, front page, or a
  spinning disc ringed by a live spectrum.
- **Visuals** — generated scenes that hit on the bass: a purple nebula with neon comets, a voxel landscape with a
  river, the artwork as a spinning vortex, a spiky chrome sculpture, a globe of dots, a hill of glowing strands
  with a ring on every beat, the artwork smeared into motion blur, a warm glow, black and white contour bands,
  molten seams, a topographic map, folding silk, ink on paper, a neon tunnel, a wireframe ocean, a night city
  flyover, a ringed planet, a shoal of fish, a warp-speed starfield, a paper bird over hills, a freefall between towers, a hall of
  glowing frames, a wall of old TVs, a neon lattice, northern lights, neon dunes, a glowing lagoon, canyon
  strata, a helix of blocks, a clock with butterflies, a glass swirl, a gravity well and wood grain. **Visual Mix** cuts between
  them every couple of lines, with a different one on each screen.
- **Live wallpaper** — the whole look as your desktop wallpaper on every screen while music plays, behind your
  windows and icons; your own wallpaper comes back when the music stops.
- **Auto** cuts between them like an editor: on the drop, on new sections, every few beats in the chorus.
- **Hears the music** (Core Audio process tap): zooms, flashes and particles kick on the real beat.
- **The artist stands in front of the clock** — the person is cut out of the art with Vision.
- **Edge light** — racing streaks or a full rim around the screen, in album colours, white or your own, with
  thickness and brightness controls. It can move with the music or hold still, and can run on the desktop too.
  It's on by default and stays as you set it whichever preset you pick; turn it off with the 💡 button on the
  lock-screen bar, **Edge Light** in the menu bar, or in Settings.
- Particles (bokeh, stars, embers, dust, snow, rain, petals, fireflies, lanterns, comets, bubbles), light leaks,
  stage beams, VHS look, RGB split, edge glow, and a backing for readability.

- **Reel lyrics** — poster type built word by word, with slams, rises, echoes, colour blocks and blackouts.

**Presets** — eighty-eight looks in one click, in groups (Visualizer, Nebula, Voxel Land, Chrome, Dot Globe, Particle Hill, Fan Edit, Cinema Cut, Poster Drop, Reel Edit, Late Night, Fisheye, Fisheye Visual, Night Drive, Hype, Chill,
Love Letter, Glitchcore, Movie Subs, Galaxy, Highlighter, Sad Hours, Golden Hour, Y2K,
Polaroid Days, Mixtape, Moonlit, Late Show, Synthwave, Snow Globe, Monsoon, Sakura, Festival, Bubblegum, Stadium,
Ripple, and 24 more, from Art Gallery to Zen Garden), plus your own. Switch from the menu bar, or on the lock screen itself: ✨ opens the list, and the arrows beside it step through them.
Heart your favourites to put them first and keep the arrows among them, and pin a preset to a song or artist
to have it come back whenever they play.

**Getting the lyrics right**
- Lyrics come from [LRCLIB](https://lrclib.net) (free, no account). Uploads are user-submitted, so Cadence
  picks the one whose timing agrees with the rest.
- **Every source at once** — LRCLIB, NetEase, KuGou and lyrics.ovh race, so the first good answer shows
  straight away, and a better one replaces it. A source that's down is skipped for two minutes.
- **Saved lyrics** — timed lyrics are kept on disk, so songs you've played load instantly and work offline.
  Failed searches retry by themselves every 30 s for 3 minutes.
- **Plain lyrics** — when only untimed words exist, they're spread over the song with estimated timing, ready
  for Tap to Sync.
- **Auto-fix timing** — lines up when singing starts in the audio with when lines start, and shifts the
  lyrics when they're clearly early or late.
- **Word timing** — real word-by-word timing from NetEase where it exists. Otherwise Cadence records the singing
  band as a song plays, works out where each line and word really falls, and uses that on later plays.
- **Matches your ears** — holds lyrics back by your output device's delay, which is large on Bluetooth headphones,
  and starts slow word effects sooner than quick ones so they all land with the voice.
- **Lyrics repair** — when the timed upload is missing lines or mangled, Cadence rebuilds it from the
  complete untimed lyrics, borrowing the timestamps.
- **Your fixes stick** — nudge a song's timing (menu bar → This Song's Timing) and Cadence remembers it for
  that song. For a truly broken one, **Tap to Sync**: play it once, press Space as each line starts, and
  your timing is used from then on.
- Songs without timed lyrics get a visual mode: the title over a live spectrum.

**YouTube** — YouTube and YouTube Music in the browser work like a player app:
- The song and artist come from YouTube Music, the "Music" card under a video, or the video title, read
  every likely way ("Artist - Song", "Song | Film | Singer", "Artist 'Song' MV") with "Official Video" and the
  like dropped. Each reading is searched, and only one that matches a real song's title and artist finds anything.
- Videos run longer than songs. Lyrics timed to the video itself win when they exist; otherwise the song's
  lyrics are used, and auto-fix timing searches the whole gap for where the song starts in the video.
- Ads hold the lyrics where they were, and vlogs, games and news are ignored.
- Play/pause, next, previous and scrubbing on the lock screen control the tab.
- Spotify and Music come first when they're playing. Turn it off in Settings › YouTube.

**Also** — clock, now-playing bar with controls and scrubbing, battery saver (lighter visuals when unplugged),
keeps the display awake while lyrics play, optional floating lyrics on the desktop, 21 bundled fonts plus every
font on the Mac.

## Requirements

- macOS 14 or later (hearing the music needs 14.2, mesh gradients macOS 15, the glass bar macOS 26)
- Apple silicon
- Xcode command line tools (`xcode-select --install`)

## Build and install

```sh
./build.sh
```

Compiles `Sources/*.swift`, bundles the fonts, signs the app and installs it to `~/Applications/Cadence.app`.

On first launch macOS asks to let Cadence control Spotify / Music and your browser — that's how it reads the
current song and position — and to record system audio, which it analyses on the Mac for the beat and timing (nothing is
recorded or sent anywhere). `build.sh` signs every build with a local self-signed certificate, created on the
first build by `scripts/signing-identity.sh` in its own keychain, so macOS remembers these permissions across
rebuilds.

For YouTube, the browser also has to allow JavaScript from AppleScript, once (Cadence explains this the first
time it finds YouTube open, and **Set Up YouTube…** in the menu shows it again):
- Chrome, Brave, Edge, Vivaldi: **View › Developer › Allow JavaScript from Apple Events**
- Safari: **Settings › Advanced › Show features for web developers**, then **Developer › Allow JavaScript from
  Apple Events**

## Using it

- Menu bar 🎤 → **Presets**, **Lyric Animation**, **Background**, **Layout**, or **Settings…** for everything.
- **Preview Lock Screen (10s)** shows the lock-screen view on the desktop. From a terminal:
  `swift scripts/preview.swift`.
- Lock the Mac (Ctrl+Cmd+Q) with music playing. Hover the bottom bar for playback controls and ✨ to change
  preset. You can type your password or use Touch ID as usual.
- Logs go to `~/Library/Logs/Cadence.log`.
